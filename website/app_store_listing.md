## Description
Bizzy Books is bookkeeping the way small-business owners actually talk: "You paid $450 to Ace Supply for Supplies." One sentence per transaction, and your books build themselves.

Built for two kinds of businesses:

CONTRACTING — projects, fuel logging with per-vehicle MPG and cost-per-mile, workers comp tracking, and customer documents: contracts, invoices, receipts, and warranties, all generated as PDFs with your logo.

RETAIL — inventory done the honest way. Purchases of goods for resale are recorded the day they happen but treated as an asset, not an expense. At year end, walk your locations with the built-in count screen (blank never means zero, and it autosaves so you can stop and resume), and Bizzy Books derives Cost of Goods Sold the way Schedule C wants it: beginning inventory + purchases − ending inventory. Weighted-average costing, per-location count sheets, and a units-sold report — no barcode scanners, no point-of-sale ceremony.

Both modes generate a year-end tax document PDF mapped to Schedule C categories, ready to hand to your preparer.

Your data lives in your account in the cloud — reinstall or switch phones and it's all still there.

Bizzy Books is a record-keeping tool, not tax advice. Review figures with a qualified preparer.
